## Available plans

| Plan | Price | Best for |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Free** | $0 | Trying Hermes |
| **Monthly** | Billed monthly | Flexibility |
| **Annual** | Billed yearly | Best value |
| **Lifetime** | One-time payment | Long-term users |

---

## What's included

### Free

- Basic dictation
- Limited AI formatting
- 5 triggers max
- 20 dictionary entries
- Local storage only

### Paid plans (Monthly, Annual, Lifetime)

- Unlimited dictation
- Unlimited AI formatting
- Unlimited triggers
- Unlimited dictionary entries
- Cloud sync across devices
- Vision context
- Priority support
- All future updates

---

## Choosing a plan

### Free

Perfect if you want to:
- Try Hermes before committing
- Use basic dictation occasionally
- Test if voice input works for you

### Monthly

Perfect if you want to:
- Full features without long commitment
- Flexibility to cancel anytime
- Try paid features before going annual

### Annual

Perfect if you want to:
- Best value (save compared to monthly)
- Commit to using Hermes regularly
- Uninterrupted service for a year

### Lifetime

Perfect if you want to:
- Pay once, use forever
- No recurring payments
- Long-term investment in productivity

---

## Upgrading your plan

1. Open Hermes
2. Go to **Settings → Account** or **Billing**
3. Click **Upgrade** or **Change Plan**
4. Select your new plan
5. Complete payment

Your new features are available immediately.

---

## Downgrading

If you downgrade to Free:
- Your triggers beyond the limit are disabled (not deleted)
- Dictionary entries beyond the limit are kept but inactive
- Cloud sync stops, but local data remains
- You can upgrade again to restore everything
