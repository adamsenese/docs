## Available plans

| Plan | Price |
|------|-------|
| **Free** | Free |
| **Subscription** | \$9/mo or \$90/year |
| **Lifetime Deal** | \$150 one-time |

---

## Free

Start using the voice app.

- Dictate 2000 words
- Custom dictionary and voice commands
- Private, on-device mode
- 100+ languages
- Try AI features

---

## Subscription

Pay monthly or lock in savings for the year.

- Unlimited dictations
- Custom dictionary and voice commands
- Private, on-device mode
- 100+ languages
- Access to all AI features
- Priority support

---

## Lifetime Deal

Founding Partner Special — own it forever, no recurring fees.

- Unlimited dictations
- Custom dictionary and voice commands
- Private, on-device mode
- 100+ languages
- Bring your own AI API
- Priority support

---

## Upgrading your plan

1. Open Hermes
2. Go to **Settings → Account**
3. Select your plan
4. Complete payment

Your new features are available immediately.
