## Why use the dictionary?

Hermes might mishear names, technical terms, or unique words. The dictionary lets you teach Hermes the correct spelling for words it gets wrong.

For example:
- Your name "Senese" might be heard as "Sanese"
- The framework "Kubernetes" might be heard as "Kuberneedees"
- Your company "Acme" might be heard as "Acne"

Adding these to your dictionary fixes them permanently.

---

## Adding words to your dictionary

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Dictionary">
    Go to **Settings → Dictionary** in Hermes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Word">
    Click the **+** or **Add Word** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the details">
    - **What you say**: How you pronounce the word (what Hermes hears)
    - **What you want**: The correct spelling
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The word is now in your dictionary and will be corrected in future dictations.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Examples

| What you say | What you want |
|--------------|---------------|
| "sanese" | Senese |
| "kuberneedees" | Kubernetes |
| "reakt" | React |
| "eye oh ess" | iOS |
| "my email" | john@example.com |
| "acme corp" | Acme Corporation |

### Email shortcuts

A popular use case is creating shortcuts for email addresses:

| What you say | What you want |
|--------------|---------------|
| "my email" | john.smith@company.com |
| "work email" | j.smith@work.org |
| "support email" | support@myproduct.com |

## Recording pronunciations

For words that are hard to spell phonetically, you can record yourself saying them:

1. Click **Record Pronunciation** next to a word
2. Say the word clearly
3. Hermes learns your specific pronunciation

This is helpful for:
- Unusual names
- Words with multiple pronunciations
- Accented speech

---

## Managing your dictionary

### Editing a word

1. Find the word in your dictionary list
2. Click to edit
3. Update the "what you say" or "what you want" fields
4. Save

### Deleting a word

1. Find the word
2. Click **Delete** or the trash icon
3. The word is removed from your dictionary

### Searching your dictionary

Use the search bar to find words quickly. Hermes searches both the "what you say" and "what you want" fields.

---

## Syncing your dictionary

Your dictionary syncs across devices when:
- Cloud Sync is enabled
- You're signed in to your Hermes account
- Privacy Mode is off

Changes appear on all your devices within a few seconds.

---

## Tips

- **Add words as you encounter them** — When Hermes mishears something, add it right away
- **Be specific with phonetics** — Write how it sounds, not how it's spelled
- **Use for shortcuts** — Not just corrections, but abbreviations and expansions
- **Check for conflicts** — Make sure your phonetic spelling doesn't match a common word
