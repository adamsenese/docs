## What are Notes?

Notes let you save dictations for later reference. Instead of inserting text directly into an app, you can capture it as a note—useful for ideas, meeting notes, or drafts you want to refine.

## Saving a dictation as a note

After dictating, you can save the result as a note instead of (or in addition to) inserting it:

1. **Dictate** as usual
2. **Choose "Save as Note"** from the Hermes bar or menu
3. **The note is saved** to your Notes collection

You can also configure triggers to automatically save dictations as notes.

---

## Viewing your notes

1. Open Hermes
2. Go to the **Notes** section in the sidebar
3. Browse your saved notes

Notes are displayed with:
- The content of your dictation
- When it was created
- The app you were in (if applicable)

---

## Managing notes

### Editing a note

1. Click on a note to open it
2. Make your changes
3. Changes save automatically

### Deleting a note

1. Select the note
2. Click **Delete** or press the delete key
3. Confirm deletion

### Searching notes

Use the search bar in the Notes section to find notes by content. Hermes searches the full text of all your notes.

---

## Dictation history vs. Notes

| Dictation History | Notes |
|-------------------|-------|
| Automatic — every dictation is logged | Manual — you choose what to save |
| Quick reference for recent dictations | Long-term storage for important content |

Think of history as a log, and notes as a collection of things you want to keep.

---

## Syncing notes

If Cloud Sync is enabled, your notes sync across all your devices. Create a note on your Mac, and it appears on your other devices signed into the same account.

<Note>
Notes sync is disabled when Privacy Mode is on.
</Note>
