## Dictate smarter, not harder

Hermes is a macOS app that transforms your voice into perfectly formatted text. It understands context—whether you're writing an email, chatting on Slack, or coding—and formats your words accordingly.

## Why Hermes?

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Speaks your language" icon="comments">
    Say "um" and "like" all you want. Hermes removes filler words and adds punctuation naturally.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Knows where you are" icon="eye">
    Writing an email? You get formal formatting. Chatting on Slack? Casual tone. Hermes detects your context.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Automates your workflow" icon="bolt">
    Create triggers to search the web, open apps, control windows, or run custom AI prompts—all with your voice.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Works offline" icon="wifi-slash">
    Your voice is processed on your Mac. No audio leaves your device.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## See it in action

Here's what happens when you dictate with Hermes:

**You say:**
> "hey just wanted to follow up on our meeting from yesterday um let me know when you're free to chat"

**Hermes writes:**
> "Hey, just wanted to follow up on our meeting from yesterday. Let me know when you're free to chat."

Filler words removed. Punctuation added. Context-aware formatting applied.
